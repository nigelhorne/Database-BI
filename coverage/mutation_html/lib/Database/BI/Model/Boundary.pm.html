  126: =head1 DESCRIPTION
  127: 
  128: Finds the county each point is in and returns its outline as a GeoJSON
  129: Feature, for L<HTML::OSM>'s C<add_geojson>.  The outlines come from
  130: OpenStreetMap's Nominatim reverse lookup, through the same throttled user agent
  131: as the geocoder (L<Database::BI::Model::Geocode/nominatim_ua>), so the two
  132: together never ask more than once a second.
  133: 
  134: Only the point is sent, rounded to three decimal places (about 110 metres).
  135: Every answer is cached: a point with the outline it is in, and the outline
  136: itself.  A point inside an outline already known is matched here, without a
  137: request, so many points in one county cost one lookup.
  138: 
  139: A county here is the division of a country below its states, whose place rank
  140: in Nominatim depends on the country: 12 (a US county or independent city, an
  141: English county, a German Landkreis, a French departement) unless the country is
  142: listed otherwise: 10 for India, Bangladesh, China and Spain (district,
  143: prefecture, province), 8 for Kenya.  C<ranks> adds or changes countries.
  144: Anything else Nominatim answers is taken as "no county here" and remembered as
  145: such.  A country with nothing at its rank (Australia, Brazil, Japan) gets no
  146: outlines.
  147: 
  148: Each reply names the point's country, so a point is asked at the zoom of the
  149: last country seen, and asked again when its own country wants another rank.
  150: An outline is kept with the rank it was accepted at; when C<ranks> changes, the
  151: points it covered are asked again.
