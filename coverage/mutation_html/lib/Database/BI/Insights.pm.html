  609: 
  610: Pure-arithmetic insight detection.  No LLM, no external service calls.
  611: Detects four classes of insight:
  612: 
  613: =over
  614: 
  615: =item outlier
  616: 
  617: A value beyond the Tukey IQR fence (Q1 - 1.5*IQR or Q3 + 1.5*IQR).
  618: 
  619: =item period_delta
  620: 
  621: Overall period-over-period delta between the most recent two month-periods
  622: visible in a date column.
  623: 
  624: =item top_mover
  625: 
  626: The dimension member with the largest period-over-period absolute change.
  627: Requires at least one categorical column and one date column.
  628: 
  629: =item seasonality
  630: 
  631: Detected via lag-1 autocorrelation on monthly totals.  A coefficient >= 0.6
  632: is flagged.  Requires at least 8 distinct month-periods.
  633: 
  634: =back
  635: 
  636: Every insight includes a C<view_url> that links to the chart or stats view
  637: showing the underlying data.  An insight without a clickable view_url is a bug.
